Your heartbeat changes the colour of your skin every time it beats. This page reads it through your phone camera. Nothing is uploaded; all processing happens on your device.

Choose a mode and tap Start

Face mode: sit still, face a window or lamp, fit your face in the oval. First reading in about 10 seconds.

What you are seeing

Face mode tracks tiny colour changes in the forehead and cheeks and separates the pulse from lighting changes using the POS remote photoplethysmography method. The red glow on your face is your own pulse signal, amplified so you can see it.

Fingertip mode uses the rear camera with the flash on. Cover the lens and flash gently with your fingertip. This is the classic phone pulse method and is usually steadier than face mode.

A reading only appears when the signal is clean enough. Check it against a pulse oximeter or by counting your pulse for 30 seconds.

The full Naadi app

This page is the pulse engine of Naadi, a 60-second offline screening station for India's frontline health workers. The hackathon build adds breathing rate, a stress index, anaemia risk from a white-card-calibrated eyelid photo, and spoken triage in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil or English, all running on the phone's NPU.

Prototype for the iQOO Hackathon 2026 by Haresh G. Not a medical device. Do not use it to make health decisions.
